How Marageli Works

  1. Browse. Look through Marageli Store (our own bulk products) or the Community Market (items posted by people around Ethiopia).
  2. Check the details. Every product shows its price in ETB, location, minimum order quantity, whether delivery is available, and a contact phone number.
  3. Contact the seller. To buy, call or message the phone number listed on the product — payment and handover happen directly between you and the seller (or with Marageli, for Store items).
  4. Delivery or pickup. Each item is marked as delivery-available or pickup-only — check before you order.
  5. Posting an item. Anyone can post to the Community Market via the bot's "Sell an item" option. A small listing fee applies, and every listing is reviewed by an admin before it goes live.
  6. Status labels. "On Market" / "Available" means it's still up for grabs. "Sold" / "Not Available" means it's gone — the listing stays visible for reference but can't be ordered.
  7. Be respectful. Accurate photos, fair prices, and honest descriptions keep Marageli useful for everyone.
